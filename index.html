🔥 Streak Mechanics & Rules

  • Completion Rule: A calendar day is marked complete when you finish and save your daily fitness log.
  • Rest Days Count: Taking planned rest days is crucial for muscle recovery. Selecting "Rest Day" and marking the log as complete continues your streak!
  • Consecutive Days: Consecutive calendar days build your streak count. A missing day breaks the streak.
  • No Duplicates: Selecting an existing date will edit that date's log rather than creating duplicates.
  • Honest Habit Building: Streak completion reflects your habit of daily logging, not whether you hit every macro perfectly.
💡 Pro-Tip for Fat Loss:

Do not drastically cut calories on rest days. Your muscles repair and synthesize new proteins during rest periods, which requires energy and amino acids.
